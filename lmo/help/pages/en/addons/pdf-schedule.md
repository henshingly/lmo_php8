# PDF schedule

1. [System requirements](#1-system-requirements)
2. [Download](#2-download)

![Schedule view](./assets/media/spielplan.png)

With this add-on you can create the schedule of your league as a PDF document. After installation, a further link appears below the link "Print league schedule", which creates and shows the PDF directly in the browser.

---

## 1. System requirements

- From LMO 4.0+: The add-on strictly requires the Classlib add-on with PDF class for LMO 4 by Tim Schumacher (available in the add-on archive). No additional PHP libraries are required.
