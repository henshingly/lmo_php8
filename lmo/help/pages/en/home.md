# Liga Manager Online – Help & Documentation

The **Liga Manager Online (LMO)** is a free, PHP-based software for managing sports leagues on the web. You enter the results, and the LMO automatically calculates tables, statistics, form curves and more. No third-party software, no time-consuming exports.

---

## What can the LMO do?

- ⚽ **League results & schedules** – for any sport, including knockout tournaments
- 📊 **Tables & statistics** – calculated automatically according to your rules
- 👥 **Teams & players** – including crests, links and player statistics
- 📈 **Form curves & cross tables** – visual evaluations at a glance
- 🎮 **Betting game (Tippspiel)** – flexible tipping system for your community, including entering tips retroactively
- 📰 **Ticker** – show current results as a scrolling ticker
- 🧩 **Add-ons** – extend the LMO as needed
